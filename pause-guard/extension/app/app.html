        Make it stick on iPhone

        1. Use Facebook in Safari with this extension allowed on facebook.com and messenger.com.
        2. Delete the Facebook and Messenger apps, or limit them with Screen Time → App Limits (1 minute, “Block at End of Limit”).
        3. In Screen Time, set Content & Privacy Restrictions → App Store → Installing Apps: Don’t Allow.
        4. Ask someone you trust to set the Screen Time passcode so you can’t undo it on impulse.

        Pause Guard never asks for your Facebook password, never reads or stores your messages, and never sends anything for you.
