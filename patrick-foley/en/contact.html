FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Write to me.

What kinds of documents can you edit?

Almost anything written: reports, articles, web pages, newsletters, internal communications, presentations, grant applications, theses and dissertations, manuscripts, policy or procedure documents, letters and résumés.

If you’re unsure, just describe your document in the form and I’ll tell you honestly whether I’m the right person for the project.

Do you work in both French and English?

Yes. I edit and write in French and in English, and translate in both directions. I can also review a translation by comparing it with its source text.

What is the difference between proofreading and editing?

Proofreading focuses on errors: spelling, grammar, punctuation and typos. It usually happens at the end, on a nearly final text.

Editing goes further. As well as correcting, it improves clarity, word choice, sentence structure, consistency and tone, while respecting your style.

Can you rewrite an existing text?

Yes. Rewriting is the right choice when a text needs to be restructured, tightened, adapted for another audience or harmonized after being written by several people. Your ideas and intent remain at the heart of the work.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the quote request form and, if possible, attach the document or a representative excerpt. Every project is different: length, type of work, subject complexity and deadline all affect the price. You’ll receive a tailored quote with no obligation.

What are your turnaround times?

They depend on the length of the text, the work required and my schedule when you get in touch. Let me know your deadline in the form: I’ll quickly confirm whether it’s realistic, and we’ll agree on a delivery date together.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Most projects are handled entirely remotely, by email, using Word, Google Docs or PDF files. A phone call or video meeting is always possible to talk a project through.
