About

About Patrick Foley

A Montréal-based linguistic editor, translator and generalist writer, I approach every text with curiosity, rigour and respect.

My work is about helping people express their ideas with greater clarity, precision and impact. A report, an article, a web page, a manuscript: every document has a reader to persuade, inform or move.

I work in English and in French, with organizations and individuals alike, and I value working directly with clients: you always know who is reading your text and why each change is suggested.

Four principles guide my work

01Curiosity

Understanding the subject and the purpose behind every text before touching it.

02Care

Paying close attention to language, structure, terminology and detail.

03Respect

Preserving the writer’s intentions and individual voice.

04Clarity

Making ideas easier to understand, never more complicated than they need to be.

Successful writing is writing where the reader never stumbles over the form and can focus entirely on what is being said.

01

A personal approach

Working with an independent professional means knowing exactly who is reading your text. I take an interest in your subject, your readers and what you want to achieve before suggesting a single change. Every edit has a reason, and I’m always happy to explain it.

02

Two languages, the same attention to detail

I work in English and in French: editing, writing and translation in both directions. This is especially useful for Montréal and Québec organizations that speak to varied audiences, and for whom the quality of both versions matters equally.

03

A curious generalist

Nonprofit reports, web content, academic writing, corporate communications, manuscripts: I enjoy moving between worlds. That versatility comes with one simple rule: taking the time to understand each field’s context and terminology.

Why editing matters

Your message deserves the right words.

Good editing goes well beyond fixing spelling. It clarifies an idea that was getting lost, sharpens a vague term, lightens an overloaded sentence and restores consistency across an entire document.

The result is rarely noticed, and that is the point: readers no longer stumble over the form. They remember the substance.
