Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Protecting your personal information matters. This policy explains what information is collected through this website, why, and how it is protected, in accordance with Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

Information collected

When you fill out the quote request form, the following information may be collected: your name, email address, organization name (optional), the nature of your project, your preferred deadline, your message and, where applicable, the attached document.

This website uses no advertising cookies or third-party tracking tools.

Use

This information is used solely to respond to your request, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead with a project, carry out the work. It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

Confidentiality of documents

Documents you send are handled confidentially and used only for your project.

Retention

Information is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and complete the project, then securely deleted, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to communicationsecrites@gmail.com.

Person in charge of personal information

Patrick Foley, communicationsecrites@gmail.com.
