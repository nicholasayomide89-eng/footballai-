# Business Administration — study sessions

Self-contained HTML study material. Each file opens in any browser with no build
step and no network dependency beyond web fonts. Progress, quiz history and
written drafts are saved in the browser's local storage on the device — nothing
is uploaded anywhere.

## Lesson 4 coverage

- 4.1 What organizing means — structure, org chart, job design
- 4.2 The six building blocks — work specialization, departmentalization, chain
  of command, span of control, centralization, formalization
- 4.3 Authority (line, staff, functional), responsibility and delegation
- 4.4 Common designs — simple, functional, divisional, matrix, team, network
- 4.5 Mechanistic vs organic structures and the four contingency factors
- 4.6 Formal and informal organization

The assessment scores out of 20, breaks results down by topic, and lists every
missed question with the correct answer and an explanation.

## Lesson 5 coverage

Planning, organizing, leading and controlling, taught with workplace examples
from Nunavut government offices, municipal offices, small businesses and
community organizations — purchase orders, invoices, budgets, scheduling,
resident complaints, record keeping and sealift logistics.

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction — what management means; manager, supervisor and employee responsibilities |
| 2 | Planning — goals, tasks, deadlines, budgets, resources, risks, backup plans; sealift ordering example and a *Think Like a Manager* question |
| 3 | Organizing — assigning, delegating, scheduling, procedures, records; interactive assign-the-work activity with feedback on all 25 task/employee combinations |
| 4 | Leading — instructions, communication, motivation, conflict, feedback, decisions; disputed-report scenario |
| 5 | Controlling — measure, compare, act; the $1,105 requisition against a $1,305 invoice |
| 6 | The four functions as a cycle — clickable diagram ending in *improve the plan* |
| 7 | Nunavut case study — Monday-morning priority ordering plus four written questions with detailed feedback |
| 8 | Management decision game — 5 scenarios × 3 decisions, each explained by strength and function |
| 9 | Practice quiz — 5 multiple choice, 2 true/false, 2 scenario, 1 short answer; shuffled, unlimited attempts, scored out of 10 with percentage bands |
| 10 | Written assignment (300–500 words) with a 20-mark rubric and a self-check list |
| 11 | Flashcards — 15 key terms, flip to reveal, mark as known |
| 12 | Printable study sheet — definitions, examples, key points, common mistakes, exam review questions |

Interface: dashboard with progress bar, sidebar navigation (Dashboard, Lessons,
