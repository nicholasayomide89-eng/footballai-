# Mechanical Engineering — study sessions

Self-contained HTML study sessions. Each file opens in any browser with no build
step and no network dependency beyond web fonts: a timed session clock, the
study notes, and a multiple-choice assessment with instant feedback and a
scored review.

| Lesson | Topic | Length | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Forces, moments and static equilibrium](lesson-01-statics.html) | 20 min (12 study + 8 assessment) | 20 |

## Lesson 1 coverage

- 1.1 Scalars and vectors, SI units, and the idealizations of statics —
  particle, rigid body, concentrated force, transmissibility
- 1.2 Forces, resolution into rectangular components, and resultants by the
  method of components
- 1.3 Moment of a force (M = F·d), Varignon's theorem, and the couple
- 1.4 Supports and their reactions, the free-body diagram, two-force members
- 1.5 Coplanar equilibrium (ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0) and static determinacy
- 1.6 Worked example — reactions on a simply supported beam carrying a point
  load and a uniformly distributed load

The assessment scores out of 20, breaks results down by topic, and lists every
missed question with the correct answer and an explanation.
